# Literature Matrix

The matrix is designed around the project's actual research questions rather than publication year.

## Axes

- SRAM topology
- leakage mechanism
- read/write decoupling
- stability
- low-voltage capability
- variation
- CIM capability
- computation type
- implementation technology
- simulation/measurement method
- relevance to our methodology

## Key comparison questions

### Cell-level
- Does the paper use 6T, 8T or another topology?
- What is added relative to 6T?
- Which operation is decoupled?
- What happens to area and leakage?

### Leakage-level
- Is leakage reduced through topology, VTH, body bias, power gating, supply manipulation, or device technology?
- Does the technique affect delay or stability?

### CIM-level
- Is computation Boolean, binary MAC, multibit MAC, analog, digital, current-mode, voltage-mode, or time-domain?
- Does the design require ADC/DAC?
- How are variation and sensing margin handled?

### Capstone-level
- Which idea can realistically be reproduced in a student Cadence/Spectre environment?
- Which claims require technology-specific devices or full macros and therefore should remain literature context?
